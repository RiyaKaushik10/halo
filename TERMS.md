## Shredding is permanent

Shredding is designed to make files impossible to restore. Halo asks you to review every shred in a confirmation sheet and to press and hold a button before anything happens, and it never shreds anything you did not explicitly confirm. Once a file is shredded, neither Halo nor its developer can recover it. Please make sure you have backups of anything you want to keep.

## Honest limits

Overwriting cannot guarantee removal on every setup — in particular on SSDs and flash drives, on copy-on-write filesystems, and where copies exist in backups, snapshots, sync services, swap or other apps. Halo explains these cases before you shred (see Help › Shredding and SSDs Explained). Halo does not provide certified data destruction, and its results must not be relied upon as proof of erasure for legal or regulatory purposes.
